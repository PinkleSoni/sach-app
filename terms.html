Last updated: October 2, 2026

In short: Use Sach honestly, write reviews about products you've actually used, and don't post anything abusive or fake. The app's match scores and ingredient flags are a helpful guide, not medical advice — always check the real label, especially for a serious allergy.

What Sach is

Sach helps you check a beauty or personal-care product against ingredients and values you've told it to watch for, using ingredient data from your own entries, from other Sach users, and from Open Beauty Facts, an open, community-run database. It also lets signed-in users write and read reviews about products.

Accounts

You can scan, check and browse without an account. Signing in (with Google) is only needed to post a review or add a product others will see. You're responsible for what happens under your account, and for keeping access to it secure.

Content you post

Reviews and products you add are yours — you keep the rights to what you write. By posting, you give Sach permission to show it to other users of the app, which is the whole point of posting it. In return, please:

  • Only review a product you've actually used, honestly.
  • Don't post anything abusive, harassing, hateful, or illegal.
  • Don't impersonate anyone, or post someone else's private information.
  • Don't spam, or add fake or deliberately misleading product entries.

Content that breaks these rules can be removed, and repeated or serious violations can get an account suspended.

Not medical advice. Match scores and ingredient flags are based on ingredient lists that may be incomplete, outdated, or entered by hand by another user — they can be wrong. Sach is a helpful first check, not a substitute for reading the actual product label or for professional medical advice, especially if you have a serious or life-threatening allergy.
