Privacy Policy for Sach

Last updated: October 1, 2026

In short: Sach doesn't have a server. Nothing you type, scan, photograph or record leaves your phone. There is no account, no analytics, and no ad tracking anywhere in the app.

What the app stores, and where

Everything Sach saves — your fit profile, your reviews, your recent checks, your wishlist, which reviews you marked helpful — is stored only in the app's local storage on your device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, because the app has no backend server to send it to.

Camera

Sach asks for camera access to:

  • Scan a product's barcode, which is read and matched on your device — the photo frame itself is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Take a photo to attach to a review you write. That photo is saved only on your device, as part of that review.

Photo library

If you choose a photo from your gallery instead of the camera — to scan a barcode in an existing photo, or to attach a photo to a review — Sach reads only the photo you pick. It doesn't browse or access the rest of your library.

Microphone

Sach asks for microphone access only if you choose to record a voice note with a review. The recording is saved on your device as part of that review and is never uploaded.

App updates

Sach checks Expo's update service to see if a newer version of the app is available, the same way most apps check for updates. That check doesn't send any of your personal data — no profile, no reviews, no photos.

Third parties

Sach does not include any advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not share data with any third party, because it doesn't collect any data to share.

Children's privacy

Sach is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, since it does not collect information at all.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored, since all of it lived only on your device. There is nothing left on a server to separately delete.

Changes to this policy

If what the app stores or asks permission for ever changes, this page will be updated, with the date above reflecting the most recent change.
