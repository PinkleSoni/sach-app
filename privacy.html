Privacy Policy for Sach

Last updated: October 3, 2026

In short: Your fit profile, scans, history, wishlist, photos and voice notes stay on your phone. If you sign in with Google and post a review or add a product, that review or product is saved on our server and anyone using Sach can read it. There are no ads and no analytics in the app.

What stays on your device

These are stored only in the app's local storage on your device and are not sent to us:

  • Your fit profile (the ingredients you avoid, values, and skin and hair conditions you select).
  • Your recent checks, wishlist, and which reviews you marked helpful.
  • The photos and voice notes you attach to a review. These are never uploaded, even when the written part of the review is shared.

What is shared when you sign in

You can use Sach without an account. Signing in with Google is needed only to post a review or add a product for others. Sign-in is handled by Google and Firebase Authentication, which receive your Google account details (such as your name and email) to sign you in. Sach itself uses only your account's unique ID and your first name.

When you post a review, the following is saved in our database and is publicly readable by anyone using Sach:

  • Your star rating, the tags you picked, and any written note.
  • Your first name and your account ID.
  • The items currently selected in your fit profile, such as skin or hair conditions (for example "Eczema-prone" or "Dandruff-prone") and ingredients you avoid. This is what lets other people find reviews from "people like me". Only select what you are comfortable sharing, because your selections are attached to each review you post.
  • When the review was posted.
Skin and hair conditions can be sensitive health-related information. They are shared only if you are signed in, have them selected in your fit profile, and post a review. If you'd rather not share them, clear them in your fit profile before posting.

When you add a product that wasn't found, the barcode, brand, name, category, ingredient list you typed, and your account ID are saved and are publicly readable. Shared products are only ever shown as an answer to a scan or search, never as a browsable list.

Where shared data is stored

Shared reviews and products are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), in Google's Mumbai, India region. Google, as our service provider, processes this data and receives standard connection information such as your IP address when the app connects to it.

Product lookups

When you scan a barcode or search a product name that isn't in Sach's built-in catalog, the barcode or search text is sent to Open Beauty Facts, an open, community-run database, to look the product up. No account details or fit profile are sent with that request. Open Beauty Facts data is available under the Open Database Licence.

Searching Google for ingredients

On a product with no ingredient list, a "Search Google" button opens a normal Google search for that product's name in your browser, so you can copy its ingredients. Sach sends nothing else to Google. What you do on Google is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Camera, photo library and microphone

  • Camera: used to scan a barcode (the camera frame is read on your device and not saved or sent) or to take a photo for a review (saved only on your device).
  • Scanning an ingredient list: when you scan the ingredient panel of a pack, the text is read on your device and your photo is not uploaded. The first time you scan, Sach downloads the open-source text-reading engine and its English language file from outside services (jsDelivr and projectnaptha.com). Those services receive an ordinary download request, including your IP address, but never your photo or anything else from your phone.
  • Photo library: Sach reads only the photo you pick, to scan a barcode or attach to a review. It doesn't browse the rest of your library.
  • Microphone: used only if you record a voice note for a review. The recording stays on your device.

App updates

Sach checks Expo's update service to see if a newer version is available. That check doesn't send your profile, reviews or photos.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

Sach does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and does not sell your data. The only third parties involved are the ones named above: Google (sign-in and database hosting), Open Beauty Facts (product lookups), Expo (updates) and, only if you scan an ingredient list, jsDelivr and projectnaptha.com (to download the text-reading engine).

Deleting your data

  • On your device: uninstalling the app deletes everything stored locally.
  • Shared reviews and products: the app can't delete these yet. To have your reviews, products or account data removed, email the address below from your Google account's email and we'll delete them.

Children's privacy

Sach is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has posted something, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects or shares changes, this page will be updated and the date above will show the most recent change.
